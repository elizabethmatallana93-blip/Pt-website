Testimonials

Meet your coach

Hi, I'm Ellie.

I've always been an athlete. I love working out and trying new things, and for most of my life my body kept up.

About five years ago I was diagnosed with POTS and hEDS, and everything I thought I knew about training got flipped on its head. I had to learn to move all over again, at a pace my body could handle. Now that's what I teach.

My mission is to help women with chronic illness see their own strength and know that the gym is for them too. And if you're brand new or starting over, you belong here just as much.

Questions

Can I do this with a chronic illness or no experience?

Yes. Every plan adapts to you, whether you're brand new, starting over, or working around a chronic condition. Please check with your healthcare provider before starting a new exercise program.

What if I have a flare or miss a week?

Missing a day or a week won't erase your progress. On 1:1 Coaching and the Personalized Plan, every workout can be adapted for a flare day, including workouts you can do in bed, because your program is built for you and your life, however that looks.

On the Guided Program, every exercise has easier and harder options, so you can scale each session to how you feel.

What equipment do I need?

The Guided Program needs one light and one heavy set of weights. 1:1 Coaching and the Personalized Plan are built around whatever equipment you have access to, including none.

Which plan should I choose?

Choose 1:1 Coaching if you want me in your corner every week. Choose the Personalized Plan if you want a plan made for you and a monthly check-in. Choose the Guided Program if you want to start on your own time. Not sure? Book a free intro call (opens booking in a new tab) or send me a message (opens Instagram messages in a new tab).

How do payments and cancelling work?

Plans are billed monthly by card through a secure checkout. To cancel, message me (opens Instagram messages in a new tab) at least 7 days before your next billing date. 1:1 Coaching at $297/mo is a 3-month plan. Full details are in the refund & cancellation policy.

What is Small Steps Society?

The community that comes with every plan: a supportive place to share wins and ask questions, bonus workouts and habit ideas, and a monthly newsletter with recipes, fun workouts and habit tips.
