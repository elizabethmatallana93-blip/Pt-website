Refund & cancellation policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

I want cancelling to be simple and clear. Here's how it works.

How to cancel

To cancel any plan, send me a message (opens Instagram messages in a new tab) at least 7 days before your next billing date. I'll confirm once your plan is cancelled.

What happens after you cancel

You won't be billed again. You keep access to your plan until the end of the period you've already paid for.

1:1 Coaching, 3-month plan

The $297/mo rate is a 3-month plan, billed monthly. If you need to stop early because of your health or a change in your circumstances, message me and we'll find a fair solution together. 1:1 Coaching is also available month-to-month at $327/mo.

Refunds

Payments are refundable only if you gave at least 7 days' notice before your billing date, or in special cases, such as a health emergency or a billing error. If you think a refund applies to you, message me and I'll review it.

Payment plans

Need a different setup for 1:1 Coaching? Ask, and we'll find something that works.

Contact

Questions about billing or cancelling? Please send me a message (opens Instagram messages in a new tab).
